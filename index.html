Engineer by training, administrator by practice

I began teaching in 2009 as a Lecturer at Government Polytechnic College, Jawad, and joined GITS Udaipur in 2013. GITS is an autonomous, AICTE-approved institute affiliated to Rajasthan Technical University, Kota, with NAAC 'A' grade.

For nine years I led the Examination Cell. Since 2023 I have served as Assistant Registrar, handling admissions, recruitment support and national data submissions such as NIRF, AISHE and AICTE EoA. Alongside, I teach, supervise M.Tech. research, and build software that automates the institute's manual administrative work.

My doctoral thesis studied how Green Lean techniques can reduce energy, resource and operational waste in the marble mining industry of Rajasthan.
